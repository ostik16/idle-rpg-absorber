# Game data

All game content lives here as JSON tables (design [5.1](../design/05-dev-tools.md)). Changing a number here changes the game. Run `npm run validate:data` after editing.

Rules: every table except `constants.json` is an array of entries with a unique string `id`. Tables refer to each other by id.

| File | Design | Fields to include (from the design's **Data** lines) | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| `constants.json` | all | global constants. `null` means not decided yet. Sections `stats`, `progression`, `enemyScaling`, `proficiency`, `potions`, `creation`, `returnTriggers`, `restock`, `cityHealing` hold the core numbers, `balance` holds the reference build the balance tools use, `engine` the simulation limits, `save` the autosave interval and how long a new page waits for the save lease | decided and default values filled |
| `items.json` | [2.1](../design/02-items-and-city.md), 2.6, 2.8 | the single items table: id, name, category (equipment, consumable, material, rare, valuable), size, stackable, requiredLevel, baseStats, bonusAttributePool (attribute, min, max, chance), buyPrice (null = not purchasable), sellPrice, icon. Equipment also `equipmentType`, `upgradeGroup` (null for a backpack), weapons `scaling` (attribute, requiredValue), backpacks `bagCells` and `maxStack`. Consumables have `effect` (potion: heal-over-time with a `tier` from `constants.potions.tiers`, plus autoUseDefaultThreshold; buff: stat, amount, durationSeconds). Rare items have `use` (equip, upgrade, consume), `effect`, `dropSources` (boss or cursed object ids), and `equipmentType: "special-item"` when equipped | slice filled (Mossfang Glade) |
| `equipment-slots.json` | 2.2 | slot list, allowed categories per slot, weapon scaling (attribute, requiredValue, penaltyCurve) | slice filled (Mossfang Glade) |
| `shops.json` | 2.5 | id, purpose, itemList, prices. Rare goods: rotationInterval, itemPool with weights, priceRange, itemsPerRotation | slice filled (Mossfang Glade) |
| `upgrades.json` | 2.7 | per upgrade group: id, itemGroup, level 1 to 9, goldCost, materials (list of itemId, amount; empty below `constants.upgrade.materialsStartAtLevel`, 1 or 2 entries from it), statBonus (10% of base stats per level) | slice filled (Mossfang Glade) |
| `areas.json` | [3.1](../design/03-world.md) | id, name, theme, levelRange, enemies (enemyId, spawnWeight), miniBoss/boss/cursedObject spawn chances, distanceFromCity (seconds), optional movementSecondsBetweenEnemies (overrides `constants.farming`) | slice filled (Mossfang Glade) |
| `teleporters.json` | 3.2 | destinations, fees | empty |
| `enemies.json` | 3.5 | id, name, level, type, health, damage, attackSpeed, defence, traits (id, value, magical), targetingMultiplier (1 to `constants.farming.targeting.maxMultiplier`; normal 2, mini-boss and boss 10), xpReward, moneyRange, dropTableId, areas. Baseline stats by level are in `constants.enemyScaling` | slice filled (Mossfang Glade) |
| `drop-tables.json` | 3.8 | id, entries (itemId, baseChance 0..1, minQuantity, maxQuantity), optional guaranteedPools (id, entries of itemId, weight, minQuantity, maxQuantity): every kill yields exactly one entry from each pool, chosen by weight, not level modified | slice filled (Mossfang Glade) |
| `attributes.json` | [4.2](../design/04-characters-and-progression.md) | id, name, feeds. Derived stat coefficients are in `constants.stats` | four decided attributes |
| `xp-table.json` | 4.3 | id, level, xpToNext (XP to go from that level to the next), levels 1 to 30 | default values filled |
| `spells.json` | 4.5 | id, kind, requiredLevel, manaCost, cooldown, effectPerRank, maxRank, rareItemPerRankAbove10 | slice filled (Mossfang Glade) |
| `proficiencies.json` | 4.6 | id, name, trainedBy. Gain per action, the diminishing curve, effect per point and title thresholds are in `constants.proficiency` | six skills named, each with a training action |
| `traits.json` | 4.7 | id, name, effect, magical | slice filled (Mossfang Glade) |
| `bestiary-thresholds.json` | 4.8 | kills needed per reveal | decided |

The validator checks references and ranges: there is no `consumables.json` or `rare-items.json` (it fails if they exist); enemy to drop table, area and traits; drop table and pool to item ids; area to enemy; shop, upgrade material, rare item drop source and spell item ids; creation constants (starting equipment and items empty or valid); min <= max; chances in 0..1; pool weights above 0; levels inside the area range. It also checks: potion tier exists and potion requiredLevel is 1; rare items have use, effect and dropSources that are bosses or cursed objects; every item has a boolean `stackable` (no `stackLimit`) and equipment is not stackable; backpacks have bagCells and a `maxStack` above `constants.inventory.baseMaxStack`; no drop quantity is above `baseMaxStack`; starting items (`constants.creation.startingItems`, now 10 minor health potions) exist and fit in one stack; a boss or cursed object drop table has a guaranteed pool of rare items plus at least one normal roll for a non-rare item; a mini-boss has normal rolls for equipment or materials and no rare items; every upgrade material has a drop source, and each also drops from an enemy of level `constants.balance.upgradeMaterialLowLevelSourceMaxEnemyLevel` (10) or lower; weapons use `requiredValue`; every equipment item has `upgradeGroup` and every group has upgrade rows for levels 1 to 9 (gold only below the material start level, 1 or 2 material items from it); targetingMultiplier within the ceiling; at least 2 normal enemies per level in each area's range; a mini-boss has a guaranteed pool with materials, a boss one with rare items; no `guaranteed: true` entries; slot names; a valuable is stackable and costs more to buy than it sells for; the default restock list holds potions a shop sells; warehouse, return threshold, city healing and flee constants are in range.

Slice conventions:
- One items table. `category` is equipment, consumable, material, rare or valuable. Valuables (silver and gold bars) move gold between characters, because each has its own (decisions L16, L17). There is no starting gear: Rusty sword and Ragged tunic are ordinary drops and shop items, and a new character fights bare-handed.
- Potions have no level requirement (`requiredLevel` 1); higher tiers heal more. Amount and duration live in `constants.potions.tiers`. They queue when drunk (`constants.potions.stacking`). Health regeneration is built into characters (`constants.stats.healthRegen`), so potions are for emergencies.
- Stacking: items have `stackable` true or false (equipment false). Every stackable item stacks to one number: the equipped backpack's `maxStack` (Canvas pack 40, Leather pack 60), `constants.inventory.baseMaxStack` (20) with no backpack, and `constants.storage.maxStack` (100) in the warehouse.
- Starter items: `constants.creation.startingItems` is 10 minor health potions; there is no starting gear.
- Balance principle: low-level enemies drop what high-level characters need. The drop-rate level modifier is 0% for a character 10 or more levels above the enemy, so upgrade materials (+6 to +9) must drop from Area 1 enemies at very low chances, which rewards running several characters. +6 and +7 materials drop at about 0.3% to 1% from normal enemies, +8 and +9 at about 0.05% to 0.3%, with higher weights and rolls from the mini-boss and boss.
- Ordinary enemies drop potions, equipment, hide scrap (a plain filler) and upgrade materials at a very low chance (0.3% to 1%). The mini-boss drops a guaranteed pool of upgrade materials plus normal rolls for equipment and materials, no rare items. The boss (and any cursed object) drops a guaranteed pool of rare items plus normal rolls for ordinary items. Rare items drop only from bosses and cursed objects. Mossfang Glade has no mini-boss or boss (decision S2), so Old Mossfang and Mossfang Matriarch list no area and no rare item can drop yet. The Leather pack (6 cells, maxStack 60) is a drop only.
- Upgrade groups: one-handed-weapon, two-handed-weapon, body-armour, light-armour (helm, boots), jewellery (necklace). Each has its own four materials: +6, +7, +8 and +9 (+9 uses the +8 and +9 materials). Materials for +8 and +9 drop very rarely in Area 1 (see the balance principle).
- `areas.distanceFromCity` is seconds of walking (10). `movementSecondsBetweenEnemies` may override the farming constant per area; Area 1 does not set it.
- The rare goods shop is not in the slice.
See decisions S1 to S8 and the owner changes that followed.
