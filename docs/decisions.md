# Decisions

Defaults chosen to close the open questions and inconsistencies in `docs/pre-development-checklist.md`. Owner-reviewed rows are in the design docs' **Rules** without the default marker. Anything not reviewed keeps "(default, revisit after playtesting)". Numbers are also in `data/constants.json` where it is a number. The owner has reviewed them: Status shows what the owner approved unchanged, decided (changed or answered), or still has open.

## Balance notes

For content writers.

- **Low levels feed high levels.** A character 10 or more levels above an enemy gets a drop level modifier of 0 (3.8), so it cannot farm that enemy. Write drop tables so that high-level characters still need what low-level enemies drop. Every upgrade material has at least one source among enemies of level 10 or below (`balance.upgradeMaterialLowLevelSourceMaxEnemyLevel`, enemy level). Players then run several characters: low-level ones farm for the rest (T12).
- **Check it in the data.** For each upgrade material, list its sources by enemy level. If none is at level 10 or below, the material breaks the principle.
- **Boosts do not help.** A drop-rate boost multiplies after the level modifier, so it cannot bring a 0 back (Q5).
- **Automation off (A1) changes the pacing.** A new character no longer turns home at 20% health: it fights on, drinks its 10 starting potions and dies, then waits in the city for the player (about 180 s of regeneration from 10%). Offline, farming stops at the first death. Potions are bought by hand. So for #37: "level 5 in about 30 minutes without getting stuck" now needs the player to resend after every death, deaths are the main cost of a session, and the death rate and potion use per hour are the numbers to watch. Check whether the 7.7% health loss per fight at level 1 (N6) makes the first deaths come too soon.
- **Healing has three tiers.** Regeneration is slow (T10), potions are fast and cost gold (2.6), and death costs respawn time plus XP (T11). Keep regeneration slow enough that potions stay worth buying.
