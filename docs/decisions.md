# Decisions

Defaults chosen to close the open questions and inconsistencies in `docs/pre-development-checklist.md`. Owner-reviewed rows are in the design docs' **Rules** without the default marker. Anything not reviewed keeps "(default, revisit after playtesting)". Numbers are also in `data/constants.json` where it is a number. The owner has reviewed them: Status shows what the owner approved unchanged, decided (changed or answered), or still has open.

## Needs the owner

Open items. Nothing blocks work.

- **I1, endurance.** The owner asked "endurance vs dexterity?". Dexterity is a different thing, so the table is a clarification, not an answer. Until decided, endurance has no effect and is off the skill list.

| Name | What it is | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| Dexterity | Attribute, raised with character points | Attack speed, critical chance |
| Speed | Hidden skill, trained by moving (I2) | Shorter travel and between-enemy time |
| Endurance | Visible stat, formerly the travel-speed skill | Nothing now: redundant with speed |

  Question: drop endurance, or give it another job (for example a stamina or resistance role)?
- **Respawn unit.** Level x 2 minutes was assumed (I4). At level 30 that is 60 minutes. Minutes or seconds?
- **No starting potions.** C2 says no starting items; this is assumed to include potions.
- **Shop refresh cost.** 5,000 gold to force a rotation is the lead's default (Q3). Review it.
- **Cursed objects drop rare items.** Assumed to stay (S8), while mini-bosses now drop upgrade materials only.
- **Weapon penalty scope.** "1% from the stats" is assumed to be 1% of the weapon's own damage, not of the whole damage total (Q2, 4.2).
- **Other assumptions to confirm:** a backpack's stack multiplier may push material stacks above the 100 cap (`storage.materialStackSize`);  the slot ticket is applied automatically (T4); targeting multiplier applies to movement time only (T5); a forced shop rotation restarts the hourly timer (T8); backpack stack multiplier applies to that character's bag only (T1); boost equipment is normal equipment (T9); XP loss is 25% of the level's earned XP, limited by the floor (T6).
- The numbers above are guesses to tune with the balance tools (5.3). N1 to N12 assumed about 12 seconds per kill; movement is now 6 seconds, so the XP table may be a little generous.
